## Executive result

The official OLMo-3.1 Think-SFT/Think-DPO wedge does not localize the first
released Think transition under the frozen assay. Both checkpoints fail the
prospective Bank-S direct+composed capability cohort, so the planned
intervention effects are missing rather than zero. The joint route is
`null_or_unresolved`; the conditional Tier-2 lens refit is forbidden.

The mandatory finite-dose H6 validation also finds no licensed in-band regime
at layers 24, 32, or 40 for Base or OLMo-3.1 Think on the frozen epsilon
ladder. Think alone passes the layer-56 late anchor at epsilon 0.10. Exact
intervention-dose coverage cannot be computed from the registered archive and
remains unavailable rather than zero.

Finally, the outcome-blind future Think/Instruct Bank-W pair ruler finds 16
shared capable families and power 0.7788 at the frozen SESOI, below the 0.80
target; 18 common families are required. That planning near-miss authorizes no
intervention and provides no externalization result.

## 5. Registered-dose archive audit

The frozen join requires exact `(model, item_id, layer, position)` rows with
total removed-energy fraction and the local residual-norm conversion. Six live
relevant Phase-3/4 tables were verified. None contains the complete key/value
set. Per-item energy means/maxima and per-position protected-subspace energy
are not substitutes for total site dose.

Consequently, intervention-dose coverage, the causal epsilon distribution,
and whether epsilon 0.10 exceeds a typical delivered dose are unavailable.
They are not zero. No post-outcome reinstrumentation was performed.

## 6. Future OLMo-pair power ruler

The pair analysis was committed before reading the exact Think/Instruct
intersection. It uses a two-model shared-family max-T statistic, one shared
sign per family, alpha 0.05, Student-t(5) heavy tails, 5,000 simulations, and
2,048 sign-flip draws. The existing substantive ruler is unchanged:

- SESOI: 0.10 nat per doubling, or 0.15849625 nat from load 2 to 6;
- family SD: 0.23 nat, the largest registered common-family Bank-S SD rounded
  upward to 0.01 nat;
- target power: 0.80.

Think and Instruct each pass 17 families, but share 16. All four frozen null
scenarios have calibrated type-I rates (0.0486, 0.0524, 0.0514, and 0.0508).
Power at 16 is 0.7788; the first count meeting target is 18. Event
`ol2-bank-w-olmo-pair-power-v1` therefore records
`not-powered-at-current-support` and `intervention_authorized=false`.
