were stable; the prompt-112 control showed that the mismatch was broader than
one prompt. Nominal versions and model bytes matched, but historical
distribution-content and compiled-kernel identities were unavailable.

amendment fixed exact current distribution contents, a primary computation,
a discarded repeatability computation, and the unchanged 0.5 within-runtime
norm tolerance before any new influence output. It changed no bank, model,
lens, endpoint, sample, materiality threshold, or SESOI.

per-layer difference is 0.004572. All tensors are finite. The A500 and A1000
materiality values are tiny, with the closest more than 3,800-fold below its
