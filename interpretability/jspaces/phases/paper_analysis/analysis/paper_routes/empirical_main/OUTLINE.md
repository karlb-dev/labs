Title family: *Verbalizable Causal Channels Are Model- and
Task-Dependent in Open Language Models*.

load-bearing knowledge-access channels whose effects are carried by
direction content rather than dose, bridge-mediated on Qwen, and
training-checkpoint-dependent on the OLMo lineage.
