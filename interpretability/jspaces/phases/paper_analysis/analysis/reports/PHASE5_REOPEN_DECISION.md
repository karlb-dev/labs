## Why no candidate clears criterion 10 ("worth delaying publication")

The route decision gives each paper a thesis that is *complete on the
frozen record*: Paper A's causal core is confirmatory + replicated as
it stands, and every open edge (externalization, stage localization,
substitution semantics) is licensed as *open* rather than resting on a
pending result. Paper B's thesis **is** the boundary map — running new
experiments to soften the boundaries would weaken the paper's central
lesson, not strengthen it. No candidate changes a central sentence of
either paper from "open/gated" to "required for the thesis".
