# Phase 5 horizon

Status: sanctioned groundwork only. No Phase 5 evidence namespace opens
before the Phase 4 freeze requirements, independent review, PI signature,
freeze commit, and tag are complete.

## Entry routes

### 5A — Circuit completion

Entry criterion: P4-P1b rejects the bridge-versus-answer-direction null on
untouched families. Work then moves from a causal bridge-state claim to its
receiver route: path localization, receiver patching, cross-relation
transfer, wrong-layer and unrelated-patch controls, and the first positive
receiver slice anticipated by Phase 4 §7.6.

### 5B — Working-memory characterization

Entry criterion: P4-P3's multiplicity-controlled joint max-T primary rejects,
the capability guard passes, and the load effect replicates on held-out
families. Work then estimates full load curves, capacity-to-behavior coupling,
derivation/redundancy interactions, delay and interference surfaces, and the
scope of the earned term “load-dependent working-set channel.” A null or
capability-confounded result retains “knowledge-access channel.”

### 5C — Cross-family prediction test

Entry criterion: Phase 4 freezes; this route is desirable regardless of
P4-P1/P4-P3 sign if budget permits. Select one untouched open lineage with a
base/post-trained pair from a Llama-class or Mistral-class family. State the
training-installs-organization prediction before any cell runs, then apply
the frozen transport gate, lens-convergence protocol, span-safe causal assay,
exact controls, capacity estimator, and common-support analysis unchanged.
This is the highest-credibility conversion from postdiction to prediction.

### 5D — Methods release

Entry criterion: Phase 4 completion. Package the per-checkpoint transport
gate, nested-corpus convergence protocol, span-safe selection, exact
profile-consuming control, corrected capacity estimator, event-sourced
provenance, and reproduction checks as a standalone artifact or methods note,
decoupled from the broader workspace interpretation.

## Publication routing

The standing default is a single paper after Phase 4 freezes and the
confirmatory family is run once. The lineage trajectory plus lens-convergence
study strengthen that option. Unless P4-P1b lands cleanly, 5A and 5B remain
future work and 5C becomes the next paper's predictive spine. If P4-P1b does
land cleanly, the receiver mechanism can carry the current paper's mechanism
section while 5C remains the sequel.

## Governance

- No known-bank development result becomes a Phase 5 binary primary.
- Entry criteria are evaluated from registered Phase 4 evidence only.
- Untouched families, hashes, seeds, aliases, and branch rules freeze before
  any Phase 5 outcome.
- A failed entry criterion routes to its named downgrade; it does not trigger
  outcome-driven task, endpoint, or threshold replacement.
