in flight from the durable n=554 recovery boundary. At this timestamp no

## 1. Purpose

The A250-to-A500 development result established that convergence of the
averaged transport operator need not imply convergence of literal sparse
token-row selections or every causal endpoint. This amendment prospectively
defines the scientific object for the narrow Q-L2 outcome in which literal
row identities continue to drift at A1000 while the selected span and all
load-bearing causal quantities are stable within the already frozen
tolerances.

This document does not weaken any A1000 gate after seeing its result. It
states how Phase 4 will interpret one branch before that result is available.

## 2. Conditional estimand

If and only if the mechanical A1000 decision resolves to Q-L2, the primary
sparse intervention object becomes the **selected transported span at each
frozen layer and position**, represented by a numerical-rank-safe
orthonormal basis and its projector. Scientific comparisons concern:

- the span projector and principal-angle distribution;
- protected-span overlap and delivered numerical rank;
- removed energy and dose fidelity;
- capacity and positive-control behavior; and
- the frozen aggregate causal and bridge endpoints induced by that span.

Literal vocabulary-row membership remains recorded in full but is demoted to
a methods diagnostic. A selected-ID difference does not by itself imply a
different scientific intervention when disputed rows are aliases or
near-tied directions whose spans and induced causal quantities satisfy the
frozen gates.

This amendment does not license a claim that the token dictionaries are
identical. The scoped claim would be that the A1000 span-level instrument is
stable despite redundant or underidentified row labels.

## 3. Mechanical activation gate

The amendment activates only when every condition below holds for the frozen
A500-to-A1000 primary comparison and the required A1000-to-published
sensitivity:

1. The registered A1000 fit passes its complete tensor, corpus, diagnostic,
   recovery, and provenance checks.
2. The structural gate passes: conservative assay-band task-token median
   cosine is at least `0.95`, and task-token q05 is at least `0.90`.
3. Literal selected-ID Jaccard is below its original `0.75` floor.
4. Median normalized selected-projector overlap is at least `0.85`.
5. The selection-margin audit reports the frozen stable-core, near-tie, and
   rank-deficient strata without excluding any stratum from the functional
   gate; protected rank and span geometry have no unresolved contract error.
6. Occupancy differs by at most `1.0` and centered excess differs by at most
   `1.0` percentage point at every frozen capacity landmark.
7. The absolute equal-family span-safe-specific mean difference is at most
   `0.15` nat, and the absolute tail-rate difference is at most `0.05`.
8. Both lenses pass the frozen G4 positive-control rule, and their absolute
   flip-rate difference is at most `0.10`.
9. Bridge rescue and bridge preference retain their required signs and each
   differs by at most `0.25` nat.
10. No other load-bearing causal, prose, delivery-fidelity, or structural
    dependency gate is decision-sensitive.

The numerical thresholds above are inherited unchanged from the
prospectively frozen A250-to-A500 functional contract. Point estimates that
fall within these tolerances are described as *within the frozen decision
tolerance*. Statistical-equivalence language is used only for an endpoint
whose precommitted TOST margin and power make that claim valid; correlation
or a non-significant difference is never relabeled equivalence.

If the projector gate fails, a causal endpoint is decision-sensitive, the
selection-margin audit finds a load-bearing stable-core failure, or any
contract check fails, this amendment does not activate. The result routes to
Q-L3, Q-L4, or Q-L5 under the governing Phase 4.3 table rather than being
forced into Q-L2.

## 4. Consequences if activated

- Nominate the registered A1000 lens as the Phase 4 Qwen candidate
  instrument, subject to independent review and the remaining freeze gates.
- Preserve exact selected IDs, ranks, scores, margins, and alias annotations
  as methods outputs and sensitivity analyses.
- State that sparse row labels are redundant or underidentified only to the
  degree supported by the outcome-blind margin/alias audit.
- Define later Qwen Phase 4 interventions by the registered A1000
  numerical-rank-safe selected span and exact dose/protection contract, not
  by requiring another fit to reproduce arbitrary row identities.
- Do not average, union, or hand-select rows after outcomes. Any consensus or
  cross-fit instrument remains a separate prospective design and belongs to
  Q-L3 review, not this branch.

literal-ID stability remains part of the fully passing result. If Q-L3,
Q-L4, or Q-L5 obtains, this draft is inapplicable and cannot be cited to

Activation does not sign the preregistration, open untouched data, or waive
independent protocol review, PI approval, the freeze commit, or the freeze

estimand requires a new dated amendment with an explicit supersession edge.
