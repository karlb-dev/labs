# Bank B Phase 4 decision memo

**PROSPECTIVE CANDIDATE DECISION — ESTIMATION-ONLY; INDEPENDENT REVIEW AND PI
APPROVAL PENDING**

Recorded 2026-08-02 without opening any Bank B intervention, confirmatory, or
replication outcome.

## Decision

Remove P4-P1 from the Phase 4 confirmatory primary family and retain Bank B as
an **estimation-only future instrument-development resource**. The sealed
confirmatory and replication partitions remain sealed. This decision does
not authorize an exploratory run on them.

## Basis

The registered power audit and feasibility envelope rule out an allocation
repair. At the 0.25-nat joint SESOI, the existing 10-family confirmatory side
has power 0.038 and even an optimistic known-SD, Gaussian, single-component
bound requires 3,562 independent families. Assigning all 40 existing
families to one side still has an optimistic minimum detectable effect of
2.359 nats; the registered heavy-tail intersection-union ruler requires
3 nats. Even substituting the consumed Phase 3 bridge-specific mean of
1.342 nats requires at least 124 families under the optimistic bound.

The alternatives do not repair the current Phase 4 design:

- expanding from 40 to thousands of independently sourced relation families
  is not a bounded completion path;
- raising the SESOI would be a power-driven change rather than a substantive
  claim boundary; and
- an answer-direction-orthogonal intervention changes the estimand and needs
  new outcome-blind dose, variability, and exact-power evidence. It is a
  legitimate future study, not a patch to this primary.

## Consequences

1. The compact Phase 4 primary family contains P4-P2 and P4-P3 only, subject
   to their remaining gates and power requirements.
2. Existing Bank B authoring, verification, geometry, and power events remain
   valid methods/development evidence and are not withdrawn or relabeled.
3. No Bank B confirmatory or replication outcome may be opened under the
   current preregistration.
4. A later orthogonal-rescue study requires a new evidence namespace,
   prospective estimand, consumed-development pilot, independent family
   bank, power ruler, review, and freeze before outcomes.
5. Candidate-preregistration wording may incorporate this decision, but the
   independent-review, PI, freeze-commit, and freeze-tag fields remain
   pending.

This is the least claim-inflating resolution of the registered infeasibility
result and preserves the bank for a properly redesigned future experiment.
