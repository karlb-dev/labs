## 0.1 Phase 4.4 is a decision block, not another discovery sweep

Phase 4.3 banked the expensive object:

- the exact cumulative Qwen draw-A estimator reached 1,000 accepted prompts;
- the registered fp16 lens and fp32 checkpoint pass full tensor integrity;

1. literal selected token rows;
2. selected subspace;
3. capacity and positive controls;
4. aggregate causal effects;
5. bridge-specific mechanism endpoints.

A1000 is the final automatic fit-size escalation. There is no A2000 escape hatch.

## 0.2 The Phase 4 primary family has already narrowed

| Candidate | State | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| P4-P1, bridge specificity | **Estimation-only** in candidate 0.11 | The consumed orthogonal shot is a future-instrument diagnostic. It cannot restore P4-P1 as a Phase 4 primary. |

| P4-P3, controlled working-set load | **Capability-blocked at 16/20** | No Bank-W intervention is authorized. Dropping a passing model to repair support is forbidden. |

Therefore the mainline scientific question for this VM is:

> **Can Phase 4 lawfully freeze with one powered P4-P2 primary, or should it freeze as a no-new-primary methods and development extension?**

Either answer is a successful narrowing. The block must not invent a new bank, enlarge a SESOI, add a model, or open a convenient endpoint merely to preserve the appearance of a three-primary phase.

## 0.3 Three acceptable terminal states

### Terminal A: one-primary freeze candidate

Requirements:

- Q-L1 or Q-L2;
- A1000 is a licensed canonical Qwen instrument;
- the reviewed consumed P4-P2 pilot is mechanically valid;

- P4-P1 remains estimation-only;
- P4-P3 remains blocked.

Output: a reviewable Phase 4 candidate with exactly one binary primary, P4-P2.

### Terminal B: no-primary methods freeze candidate

This occurs if Q-L1/Q-L2 holds but P4-P2 is infeasible, remains review-gated, or cannot obtain a defensible untouched design.

Output: freeze the development and methods contribution honestly:

- same-corpus operator convergence;
- measured sparse-selection and mechanism sensitivity;
- P4-P1 power failure and estimation-only disposition;
- P4-P3 capability-boundary result;
- model-dependent transport applicability;
- the already established Phase 3 causal findings remain the main empirical claims.

### Terminal C: instrument-instability methods candidate

This occurs under Q-L3, Q-L4, or Q-L5.

Output:

- no new single-lens Qwen primary;
- no Bank-B or P4-P2 lens-dependent follow-up under the current instrument;
- a methods result about the separation among operator, sparse-span, and causal convergence;
- Phase 3 published-lens results remain immutable historical evidence and are not retroactively erased;
- any consensus, cross-fit, or lens-random-effects successor becomes a separately reviewed future phase, not a same-VM rescue.

## 0.4 Scientific convergence rule

The paper does not need one more dramatic bar. It needs a stable boundary around what is already known.

The block should leave the campaign able to say one of the following, with evidence attached:

1. **A1000 is a stable Qwen causal instrument at the span and conclusion level.**

3. **The operator converges but some causal conclusions remain fit-sensitive.**
