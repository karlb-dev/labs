## 2. Amendments

**2.1 Pre-write both G2.2 outcome sentences before G2.1 runs.** The parity question and the defect question are different sizes: the tangent-versus-response mismatch is orders of magnitude larger than the backend disagreement, so under *either* G2.2 branch the study-1 scientific verdict likely survives — as an exact-JVP claim if the floor account holds, or restated within a measured backend envelope if path ambiguity holds. Commit both candidate sentences (and the third, blocked-branch sentence) to the Gemma lane's protocol directory before the calibration executes. This prevents the license decision from being narrated as a verdict reversal in either direction, and it makes the relicensing — if it comes — provably unshopped.

**2.2 The wedge predictions get the third-timestamp treatment.** §9.5's frozen stage predictions must land in the OLMo foundation event, with per-condition directional signs for the three outcomes (transition at SFT / at DPO / diffuse), *before* any wedge cell runs — and the "diffuse" outcome is pre-declared informative, not a failure: a two-stage recipe installing the effect gradually is a real finding about how recruitment forms. This is the externalization line's fourth timestamp (anticipated → observed → predicted-in-factorial → now localized-or-diffuse), and the discipline is what makes the wedge citable whichever way it lands.

**2.3 Register the ancestry qualification as evidence.** "Ancestry-qualified" must be an artifact, not an adjective: record the config/tokenizer/architecture hashes and any recipe-card statements establishing that Think-SFT and Think-DPO lie on the 3.0 Think path, as the wedge's first `ol-` event. If any field cannot be established, the wedge proceeds with the gap stated in its claim boundary rather than assumed away.

**2.4 Two frames on every wedge checkpoint.** The recruitment claim is frame-dependent by construction. Every wedge cell reports under both the frozen base-lens common frame and the 3.0 Think endpoint own-lens frame (both already registered; Tier 1 means no new fits), with the frame-agreement statistic carried per cell. A transition visible in one frame only is a coordinate finding, not a recruitment finding, and the pre-registered predictions should say which frames each account expects to move.

**2.5 In-block drop order.** If the 24 h window compresses: G2.1 and G2.2 are never dropped (the license decision is this block's export to every future transport claim); then OL2.1's seven conditions on both wedge checkpoints; then the OLMo in-band transport validation under whatever license G2.2 produced; then conditional Tier-2 refit only on its trigger; then Gemma residual G2/G5 micro-slices; the late-band micro-ladder is the first casualty. Reserve the final ~90 minutes for both lanes' bundle assembly and state-of-record pushes regardless of position in the queue.

**2.6 Model-transition hygiene.** The §8 Gemma→OLMo transition must verify GPU memory is actually released (process exit, not `empty_cache` hope), re-run the CUDA gate, and re-verify snapshot manifests on the OLMo side before the first wedge cell; a corrupted rehydration discovered mid-wedge costs more than the five-minute check.

## 3. Success criteria and the Phase 5 hand-off

The Gemma lane is done when it ships: the G2.1 calibration verdict with its measured envelope; the G2.2 license decision under the pre-written sentences; the updated licensed-sentence ceiling for the transport gate; and its bundle — which, together with study 1, completes the `TRANSPORT_GATE_PROTOCOL` export for the 5D methods release and resolves skeleton sentence 5's tier. The OLMo lane is done when it ships: the ancestry evidence; the two-checkpoint, seven-condition wedge grid under both frames with the frozen-prediction router's output (SFT / DPO / diffuse); the in-band transport validity statement at L24/L32/L40 under the lawful license; and its bundle — which upgrades or downgrades skeleton sentences 2 and 4 and hands Phase 5B/5C a localized (or honestly diffuse) mechanism target. Neither lane opens a Bank-W intervention, adds a model, or fits more than the single Tier-2 lens; the mainline's freeze does not wait on this VM, and nothing here may be cited by the freeze candidate except through admitted bundles.

## 4. Risks

(i) The calibration rabbit hole: G2.1 is bounded by its own design — if the floor cannot be characterized within its budget, the honest output is "license remains blocked, envelope measured," and the OLMo transport check ships with that caveat rather than waiting. (ii) Wedge checkpoints may fail capability more broadly than the endpoints did; the gate result is itself trajectory information — report gated-out cells as gated, never as zeros. (iii) One VM, two lanes, one clock: the §8 transition is the single most likely place to lose an hour; script it, don't improvise it. (iv) Confirmation bias runs both directions here — a team that predicted SFT will see SFT; the frozen per-condition signs and the frame-agreement statistic are the antidote, and the diffuse outcome is pre-blessed.
