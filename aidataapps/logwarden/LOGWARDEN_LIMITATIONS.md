# LogWarden limitations

- The workload is benign, synthetic, and confined to disposable SQL Server lab databases.
- The benchmark does not establish production safety or general log-analysis ability.
- Results will apply only to the frozen packets, runbooks, tools, model revisions, images, templates, and decode settings.
- Replay quality and live systems performance are different result families.
- No scientific result exists while the Tier 1 campaign remains unfinished.
